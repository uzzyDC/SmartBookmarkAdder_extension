# Privacy Policy for Mobile Bookmark Folder Finder

**Last Updated: September 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the **Smart Bookmark Adder** browser extension (the "Extension"). We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring a secure browsing experience. 

## 1. No Data Collection or Transmission
The Extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data, browsing history, or user information to external servers. It operates entirely within your local browser environment.

## 2. Permissions Used and Data Processing
To provide its core features, the Extension requests specific permissions from your browser. Here is how your data is processed locally:

*   **`bookmarks`**: Used exclusively to read your bookmark tree so you can search, filter, and organize folders. It is also used to create, move, or update bookmarks based on your direct actions.
*   **`activeTab` & `tabs`**: Used solely to retrieve the Title and URL of the currently active website tab so that the Extension can pre-fill the bookmark details and check if the current link is already bookmarked (enabling the Update or Duplicate features).
*   **`storage`**: Used to save the IDs and titles of your 3–5 most recently used folders locally on your device (the "Recent Folders" chips) so you don't have to search for them again.

**All information accessed through these permissions remains local to your device and is never uploaded or transmitted over the internet.**

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising networks. No third parties have access to your data through this Extension.

## 4. Security
Since no data is collected or transferred over the network, your bookmark data is as secure as your local browser installation. We do not have access to your bookmarks, your browser history, or your device.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or browser requirements. Any updates will be published directly on this page.

## 6. Contact Information
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or the security of the Extension, please open an issue directly in this GitHub repository.
